# MediVisit Pro Feature Expansion Plan

## For Medical Representatives (User)
1. **Interactive Product Training (LMS)**: A gamified module where MRs can complete quizzes and view interactive "Mechanism of Action" (MoA) videos to earn certifications and badges.
2. **Patient Support Programs (PSP) Enrollment**: A simple flow to register patients for support programs (e.g., dosage reminders, financial aid) directly during a doctor visit.

## For Regional Managers (Admin)
1. **Territory Performance Heatmap**: An advanced geospatial visualization tool that shows visit density and sales conversion rates across different regions.
2. **Sample Audit & Inventory Control**: A dashboard to track the chain of custody for drug samples, from the warehouse to the MR's bag to the doctor's clinic.
